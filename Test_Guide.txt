  ### Step 1: Start the Backend & Database Stack

  Run this command in your terminal from the project root (/home/chakma-
  s/Desktop/Plan-E):
    docker compose up -d --build
  Verify that all three services (plane_postgres, plane_api,
  plane_web_proxy) are healthy:
    docker compose ps

  To watch transactional logs (emails, booking events, allocation locks,
  refund hooks) in real time:
    docker compose logs -f api

  ### Step 3: Test Hotel Search, Extra Images & Room Selection Flow

  1. Switch to the Traveler Simulator tab (http://localhost/consumer or http://localhost:8000/consumer).
  2. Ensure you are on the "City Hotels" tab.
  3. Click the "Interactive Map" toggle button to view Mapbox pins for city properties.
  4. Notice the camera badge on each card (e.g. "📷 6 Photos"). Click "View Rooms & Photos" on any hotel (e.g., "Grand Metropolis Hotel" or "ITC Gardenia Luxury Hotel").
  5. In the Destination & Room Explorer Modal:
      • Tap the thumbnail strip below the hero photo to switch images, or click "View Fullscreen" for high-resolution lightbox.
      • Scroll down to "Available Rooms & Suites": inspect each room type's photo thumbnail, bed configuration, occupancy, and rate.
      • Tap any room's photo badge to open the room-specific photo gallery.
      • Select your preferred suite radio button (notice the sticky reservation bar updates rate dynamically).
      • Click "Reserve Selected Room".
  6. In Checkout:
      • Inspect the pricing breakdown: Selected Room cost + 5% Platform Fee + Lodging Tax.
      • Click "Confirm & Reserve".
  7. Expected Result: A booking confirmation popup appears with your selected room and unique reservation code (PLN-...). Docker logs show formatted booking confirmation.
  ──────
  ### Step 4: Test Resort Experience, Extra Images & Local Guide Bundling

  1. On the Traveler Simulator, toggle to the "Sanctuary Resorts" tab.
  2. Click "Explore & Rooms" on an experiential resort (e.g., "Azure Bay Ocean Resort" or "Azure Coral Bay Beach Resort & Spa").
  3. Explore resort gallery images, thumbnails, and room types.
  4. In the property view, scroll to the "Certified Local Guides" section.
  5. Click on a certified guide (e.g., Elena Silva or Tenzin Norbu):
      • Inspect their modal sheet: spoken languages, bio, daily fee, and verified badges.
      • Click "Bundle Guide with Resort".
  6. Proceed to "Reserve Experience" / "Reserve Selected Room":
      • Verify the composite price breakdown: Room cost + Guide service fee + Platform fee + Lodging tax.
      • Click "Confirm & Complete Booking".
  7. Expected Result: A composite reservation is created. The terminal log outputs both a Traveler Confirmation and an immediate Guide Dispatch Alert.

  ### Step 8 (Optional): Run the Native Mobile Client via Flutter Web

  If you want to test the native Flutter mobile client without taxing your
  CPU/RAM:

    cd mobile_app
    flutter run -d chrome

  Once Chrome launches:

  1. Press F12 to open Chrome DevTools.
  2. Press Ctrl + Shift + M to toggle Device Toolbar (select iPhone 14 Pro
  or Pixel 7).
  3. You can now test the mobile search UI, segmented control, and guide
  sheets in a mobile viewport.

  ### Resetting Test Data

  If at any point your test bookings or allocations become cluttered,
  reset the database to a clean seed state in 5 seconds with:

    docker compose down -v && docker compose up -d
